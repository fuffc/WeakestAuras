These bundled faces are unchanged copies from SharedMedia r201:

Celestia Medium Redux, DejaVu Sans, DejaVu Serif, Gentium Plus, Hack,
Liberation Mono, Liberation Sans, Liberation Serif, swf!t, and Yellowjacket.

Source: https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/sharedmedia/files/7508371
Project: https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/sharedmedia

The applicable license and attribution files are under
fonts/licenses/SharedMedia. The source registration names are retained in
TextCore.lua because WeakAuras exports store LibSharedMedia font names.
