RobotoMono.ttf
--------------
Roboto Mono, by Christian Robertson.
Licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0.
https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0

Used as the code editor's fixed-width face. A monospace font matters here for
more than looks: the editor lines up nothing by itself, so column alignment in
the user's own Lua is whatever the font gives it.

The seven bundled faces above carry the names WeakAuras2 registers them under
with LibSharedMedia ("Fira Sans Medium", "PT Sans Narrow Bold", and so on),
because a WeakAuras export stores that name and WA2Import.lua resolves it
against textCore.FONTS. Renaming one there breaks every later import silently.


Accidental Presidency.ttf, Expressway.ttf, Bazooka.ttf, FORCED SQUARE.ttf,
Cabin.ttf
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Faces that WeakAuras packs reach for through some *other* addon's
LibSharedMedia registration rather than WeakAuras' own, which is why they are
here: without them the five account for 149 of the 167 fonts the WA2 corpus
asks for and cannot get.

Accidental Presidency, by Fontourist. Freeware.
Expressway, by Ray Larabie (Typodermic). Free release, as redistributed with
  ElvUI and pfUI.
Bazooka -- public domain, per SharedMedia's own font manifest (see
  licenses/SharedMedia/font info.txt).
FORCED SQUARE, by Vladimir Nikolic. Freeware.
Cabin, by Pablo Impallari, Rodrigo Fuenzalida and Igino Marini.
  Licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1.

Registered under the LibSharedMedia names their own registrars use, for the
same reason the WeakAuras faces above keep theirs: that name is what an export
stores.
